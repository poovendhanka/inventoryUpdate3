Labour Entry: Track labour costs by selecting employees and recording their work hours and rates for a date range (e.g., weekly payments). You can set From Date and To Date to the same date for single day entries, or use a range for weekly/monthly payments. Total cost is automatically calculated based on hours worked and cost per hour.

Advance Management: You can record advances for employees and optionally apply them to labour entries. When applying advances, the system uses FIFO (First In First Out) method to adjust the oldest advances first.
